# Two budgets decide every step of a rollout

## WHY

A rolling update is arithmetic with two numbers, and every rollout bug is one of them being ignored:

- **`maxSurge`** bounds how many extra pods may exist — creation is allowed while `total < replicas +
  surge`;
- **`maxUnavailable`** bounds how many fewer than the desired count may be ready — a pod may leave
  only while `ready - 1 >= replicas - unavailable`.

The two together explain why `maxUnavailable: 0` needs a surge: with no extra pod there is nothing to
make ready before the first old one leaves, and the rollout waits forever at full capacity. It also
explains the opposite failure: `maxSurge: 0, maxUnavailable: 1` at one replica takes the service down
for the length of the swap, because the only pod that can go is the only pod there is.

## TASK

The scaffold added `src/rollout.rs` and its `pub mod rollout;` line. `available_floor` and
`may_create` are given; `may_terminate` is the stub.

- **[`may_terminate`]** — whether one more old pod may go now: the pod must exist, and after it
  leaves there must still be at least `available_floor(replicas, max_unavailable)` ready.

## DONE WHEN

- `kiln run platform/9.2-rollout` — the spec passes
- `kiln check platform/9.2-rollout` — every must gate is green
- with `maxUnavailable: 0` three pods stay three until a fourth is ready; with `maxUnavailable: 1`
  one may go immediately

## TRAP

1. **Ignoring readiness.** Counting a pod as available because it exists: the new pod is created,
   the old one leaves, and the new one has not passed its readiness probe yet — a gap the arithmetic
   was supposed to forbid.
2. **`>=` on the floor.** A pod may leave when the result *equals* the floor, not when it stays
   above it: three replicas with `maxUnavailable: 1` may run with two ready, and requiring three
   stalls the rollout at the first step.
3. **Creating past the surge.** `may_create` is the other budget; a loop that checks only the floor
   spins up pods until the cluster refuses them.
4. **A budget bigger than the workload.** `maxUnavailable: 9` on three replicas does not allow nine
   evictions; the ceiling is the number of pods that exist.
