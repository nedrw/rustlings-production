# Every step of the rollout, not just the end

## WHY

A rollout can end at exactly the right size and have taken the service down in the middle. The end
state is what the dashboard shows in the morning; the middle is what users saw. So the drill checks
**every observed step** against both budgets — the ceiling (`replicas + max_surge`) and the floor
(`replicas - max_unavailable`) — and reports the steps that broke them, not a verdict.

This is the same shape as the runbook drill (mailer 1.22): a rehearsal is evidence only when it has
somewhere to fail, and "the deployment is green" is not evidence — "no step exceeded the surge and no
step dropped below the floor" is.

## TASK

The scaffold added `src/drill.rs` and its `pub mod drill;` line.

- **[`drill`]** — for each observed step, in order: a `total` above the ceiling is
  [`Violation::TooManyPods`], a `ready` below the floor is [`Violation::TooFewReady`]; both may hold
  at one step and both are reported. Sitting exactly on a budget is allowed.

## DONE WHEN

- `kiln run platform/9.7-rollout-drill` — the spec passes
- `kiln check platform/9.7-rollout-drill` — every must gate is green
- three replicas with a surge of one and an unavailable budget of one: four total and two ready is
  allowed at the same step; five total or one ready is a violation

## TRAP

1. **Checking only the last step.** The end state is green and the middle was an outage; the spec's
   *"every step is checked, not just the last"* is the assertion.
2. **One violation per step.** Both budgets can break at once, and the reviewer is fixing two
   things; reporting the first hides the second.
3. **A strict ceiling.** Four total is *at* the ceiling, not above it; the boundary test fails if the
   comparison is `>=`.
4. **Ignoring the floor.** `maxUnavailable: 1` allows two ready out of three, so a step with two
   ready is a clean step — an implementation that requires all three fails the clean rollout.
