# Where unsafe pays for itself: a hand-rolled structure under miri and loom

## WHY

Every step before this one denied `unsafe`. `unsafe_code = "deny"` has stood in the manifest since
`5.1`, and none of them needed it — which is the right default, and the reason this step is the one
that changes it: a bounded single-producer single-consumer **ring** whose whole job is to avoid a lock
on the path between two threads. A safe version locks or allocates per message; the ring does neither.

The point of the step is not "write unsafe". It is that a structure which needs it can **argue** for
it, and the argument has exactly three obligations:

1. **initialisation** — a slot is uninitialised until a push writes it, and the queue's invariant
   (`head <= tail`, one writer at `tail`, one reader at `head`) is what makes every read sound;
2. **exclusive access** — the two threads touch different slots by the index arithmetic, and the
   indices are atomics with a `Release`/`Acquire` pair between them; that pair is the whole
   synchronisation;
3. **drop exactly once** — the `Drop` impl drops the slots between `head` and `tail` and nothing else.

Every `unsafe` block carries a `// SAFETY:` note naming the obligation it rests on, and the gate
(`unsafe-budget@5`) counts the blocks and requires the notes — which is why the number in this track's
metadata is the number of blocks in this file.

## TASK

- `src/ring.rs` — the module this step adds: `Ring<T>` over `Box<[UnsafeCell<MaybeUninit<T>>]>` with a
  `head` and a `tail` atomic, `push` (refusing a full ring and handing the value back), `pop`, `len`,
  `is_empty`, and a `Drop` that drops exactly the values in the ring.
- `Cargo.toml` — the crate-wide `unsafe_code = "deny"` comes out, and the comment where it stood says
  which step argued for the change and what replaced it.
- `tests/loom_ring.rs` — course content: the two models `loom` checks (a value published with
  `Release` seen once with `Acquire`; a slot not reused before `head` moves past it).
- `docs/unsafe.md` — the page this step ships (`links` judges it).

## DONE WHEN

```sh
cargo test -p meshkv --test spec_unsafe_deep_dive     # the six cases, including two threads
cargo test -p meshkv --test loom_ring                 # the two models, exhaustively
```

Both green, and `cargo clippy` clean with the budget in place.

## TRAP

- **`Miri` is not available on this toolchain** (`1.98.1-aarch64` ships no `miri` component — handoff
  M4 §1). The step says so: loom plus the written argument is its evidence, and the page names what is
  missing rather than implying a check that cannot run.
- **The orderings are the argument, not decoration.** `Relaxed` on the index that publishes a value
  would let the consumer read an unwritten slot — the model in `loom_ring.rs` is exactly the
  interleaving that would find it.
- **A full ring refuses; it does not block and does not drop.** The refusal hands the value back,
  because a queue that silently drops has decided something the caller did not.
- **`Drop` is where a leak hides.** A queue that forgets to drop its contents leaks silently until it
  is busy; the counter in the spec's case is the only way to see it.
- **The budget is a count of blocks, and the notes are part of the gate.** A new `unsafe` block raises
  the number in `track.toml`; a block without a `// SAFETY:` note fails the gate regardless of the
  count.

## FURTHER

- The Rustonomicon's chapters on `MaybeUninit` and on atomics are the two references this step leans
  on; the ring is the canonical exercise for both.
- `unsafe/budget@apply — jobq/3.24` asked what an `unsafe` block must *say*; this step is where one
  finally exists to say it about, and `loom` is what makes the saying checkable.
